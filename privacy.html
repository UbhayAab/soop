Privacy Notice

Dek is workplace chat software operated by your organisation. This notice explains, in plain language, what we collect and why, as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

Who is responsible

Your organisation decides why your data is processed and controls your account. Dek's operator processes it on the organisation's instructions. Contact your organisation's administrator for account questions.

What we collect, and why

  • Account details - your name, work email, and optional profile fields (title, pronouns, photo). Purpose: identifying you to colleagues. Your organisation sets who may edit these.
  • Messages, files, voice notes and reactions you post. Purpose: workplace communication and record-keeping.
  • Device and usage data - sign-in events, notification preferences, read positions. Purpose: keeping you signed in, syncing unread state across devices, and delivering notifications you asked for.
  • Crash and error reports you choose to send. Purpose: fixing defects.

Where your data lives

On managed cloud infrastructure (Supabase, Mumbai region; Cloudflare edge for files and voice relay). Access is restricted by row-level permissions to the members of your organisation.

How long we keep it

Messages and files are retained for 18 months by an automatic schedule, unless your organisation sets a different policy. Your organisation may archive or delete earlier. Backups roll off on the same cycle.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, completion, updating or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Use Settings → Your profile for most corrections, or ask your organisation's administrator, who can also erase or export your data. Grievances are answered within 90 days; contact your administrator first - their contact is on your sign-in screen.

Withdrawal and erasure

You can stop using Dek at any time; erasure of your account follows your organisation's offboarding process. Erasure requests are honoured within the retention schedule above, subject to legal hold.

Children

Dek is a workplace tool and is not directed at children. Accounts are provisioned by organisations for employees.

Security

Transport encryption everywhere, per-row access control, signed short-lived file URLs, and no third-party trackers or favicon beacons. Suspected a breach? Tell your administrator immediately - we notify affected users and the Data Protection Board without delay, with a detailed report inside 72 hours.
